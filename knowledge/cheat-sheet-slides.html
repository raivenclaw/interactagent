build-with-focus.md Module 01 — Foundations

Your starter CLAUDE.md

Drop this into any project folder. It tells Claude who you are, what stack to use, and — most importantly — what NOT to build. Prevents scope creep, keeps builds focused, stops Claude from building login screens before your app works.

Think of it as the employee handbook you give a new hire on day one. "Here's how we work. Here's what we never do."

SPEC.md Module 02 — Product Thinking

Your blueprint

Before building anything, fill this in: what it does, who it's for, what it must NOT do. Claude won't write code until you approve the spec. Prevents the boat-with-wings problem.

Like a floor plan before building a house. You wouldn't let a contractor start pouring concrete before you've agreed on where the kitchen goes.

Your page brief

Like a creative brief you'd hand to a freelance designer — except this one actually gets followed to the letter.

Module 11 — Hooks, Safety & Code Review

Your smoke
detectors.

git-safety.sh

Blocks force pushes, hard resets, and force branch deletes. The commands that can permanently destroy your work.

The childproof lock on the medicine cabinet. You can still open it — just not accidentally.

commit-quality.sh

Scans every commit for leaked API keys, debug statements, passwords in code. Catches what you can't read.

The spell-checker for secrets. It reads your code so you don't have to — and blocks anything embarrassing.

cost-tracker.sh

Logs every Claude session to a CSV. See what you're spending, find expensive sessions, set budgets.

Your credit card statement for AI. No surprise bills at the end of the month.

Plus: settings.json (wires them up) and code-review-checklist.md (5-point review for anything Claude builds).
